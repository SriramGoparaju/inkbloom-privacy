Remove Ads and Ink Drops

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. It removes banner ads and the ads shown between practice puzzles, for good. Rewarded videos stay available, but only ever when you choose to watch one for Ink Drops. It belongs to your store account, and "Restore purchases" brings it back.

Ink Drops

Ink Drops are the app's own play money. You earn them by playing — a daily claim, a Perfect Day, an optional rewarded video — and you can buy bundles of 25 or 100 as one-time purchases. You spend them on hints and other help inside a puzzle, on unlocking library packs, and on repairing a streak.

Drops are a virtual item, licensed to you for use in Inkbloom. They are not money:

  • They have no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money, sold, or transferred to another person or account.
  • A drop bundle is not refundable once its drops are credited, except where the law requires otherwise.
  • Drops are stored on your device, not in your account. They do not follow you to another device, even with a linked account, and "Restore purchases" does not bring them back. They are lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data, unless your phone's operating system restores the app's data from a backup.

Leaderboards and your display name

When you finish a daily puzzle with a Google or Apple account linked, your time goes to that day's leaderboard. Play as a guest and your times stay on your device until you link an account. Practice puzzles from the Library are never ranked, and hint-free times rank on a board of their own. A place on a board is bragging rights; there are no prizes.

If you set a display name, it appears beside your times, with your country. A name must not be offensive, pretend to be someone else, or include anyone's personal details.

Every name goes through an automatic filter for offensive words. A name that fails it is replaced by your previous name, if that one passed, and otherwise by no name at all. The filter is a simple word list, and it can be wrong in both directions. If it has refused your real name, write to us.

We may remove or change a display name that breaks these terms, and remove or hide any result we believe was not played fairly. Some of this happens automatically: a time faster than a person could plausibly solve the puzzle is refused, or kept off the top of the board.
