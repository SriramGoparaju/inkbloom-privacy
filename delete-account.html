Delete your Inkbloom account

Last updated 17 September 2026

You can have your Inkbloom account and everything stored against it deleted, at any time, for any reason. You do not need the app installed to ask.

Email sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com

Put "Delete my Inkbloom account" in the subject line. We reply to confirm, and the deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.

What to include

If you linked a Google or Apple account, simply write from that address. That is what identifies your account to us, and we need nothing else.

If you never linked an account, your play is not connected to any name, address or identity, so there is nothing we can look you up by. Uninstalling the app deletes everything held on your device. If you would also like the copy held on our servers removed, write to us and we will tell you how to find the account identifier the app is using, so we can match it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account itself, and the sign-in record behind it.
  • Your profile: the display name you chose and your country.
  • Every puzzle you have played: progress, solve times, hints used, and unfinished boards.
  • Your streak and your settings.
  • Your display name and country are removed from every leaderboard you appear on.

What is kept, and why

A leaderboard is a record of a particular day, so the times themselves stay on the board after your name and country come off them. What remains is an anonymous row that cannot be traced back to you, kept so that the day's rankings and percentiles do not silently change for everyone else.

Purchase and subscription records are held by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us, and are kept by them for their own tax and accounting purposes. We cannot delete those on your behalf, and you should contact the relevant store if you need to.

Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your name and cannot be traced back to you individually. They expire on Firebase's own schedule, which is capped at fourteen months.

Cancelling a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel an Inkbloom Plus subscription, because the subscription belongs to your store account rather than to us. Cancel it in the Google Play Store or in your Apple subscription settings first, then ask us to delete the account.
