Keep the streak

Perfect days build a streak, the only number the app asks you to care about. Miss a day and it says so kindly, once.

Plays anywhere

Every puzzle is on your phone before you open it. Plane, train or basement: no signal needed. Progress syncs when you're back online.

A Library that doesn't run out

Over two thousand puzzles across eleven games and three difficulties. The first fifteen in every track are free.

Compete only if you want to

Leaderboards are there if you go looking. You see a percentile, like "top 20% today", never a rank thrown at you.

Ads that know their place

No ad ever interrupts a puzzle, and the daily page stays clear of them. Go ad-free any time.

Easy on the eyes

Two themes, Morning Paper and Evening Ink, in warm paper tones rather than glare. Type sized for reading.
