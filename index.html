Inkbloom Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Inkbloom is a daily puzzle app. This policy explains what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

  • You can play the entire app without giving us your name or email.
  • An account is created for you automatically, and it is anonymous.
  • If you choose to appear on a leaderboard, the name you pick and your country become visible to other players. Nothing else you do is ever public.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier — see Advertising below.
  • You can ask us to delete everything, and we will.

Who is responsible

Inkbloom is published by Sriram Goparaju. For any question about this policy or your data, write to sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

An account, created automatically

The first time you open Inkbloom it signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This produces a random account identifier. It is not linked to you as a person, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. It exists so your puzzle progress survives a reinstall and can follow you to another device.

An account you link, if you choose to

You may link a Google or Apple account. We do this so your progress can be restored, and because appearing on a leaderboard requires an account that is genuinely yours. When you link one, Firebase Authentication records the email address of that account. We never copy that address into our own database and we never use it to contact you. It stays inside Firebase Authentication, and on your own device.

What you choose to show

  • A display name, if you set one. It is checked automatically against a list of offensive words, and a name that fails is replaced with your previous one. It appears next to your times on public leaderboards.
  • A country, taken from your device's region or chosen by you. It appears on leaderboards and powers the country filter.

Leave the display name empty and you rank anonymously.

How you play

We store your progress on each day's puzzles: which ones you solved, how long you took, how many hints you used, and the state of any board you left unfinished, along with your streak and your settings. This is what makes the app work across devices, and it is what the Stats screen reads.

When you finish a daily puzzle, your time is submitted to that day's leaderboard along with your display name and country. Practice puzzles from the Library are never submitted and never ranked.

Technical and diagnostic data

  • Your device's timezone offset, so a "day" means your day rather than ours, and your streak does not break at the wrong hour.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics, so a crash can be found and fixed. These include the device model, operating system version, and where in the app the failure happened.
  • Anonymous usage events through Firebase Analytics: that a puzzle was started, completed or abandoned, and with which game and how long it took; that a day was completed; that a streak reached a milestone; that an ad was shown or opted into; that a purchase screen was viewed or a purchase made; and that notifications were turned on. These are counted in aggregate to understand which puzzles people enjoy. They are tied to your account identifier, not to your name.
  • An attestation token through Firebase App Check, which confirms requests come from a genuine copy of the app. It carries no personal data and exists to stop leaderboard cheating.

Notifications

If you allow them, Inkbloom sends at most two notifications a day: one when the new page is ready, and one in the evening only if a streak of three days or more is about to end. Both are scheduled on your own device. You can turn either off in the You tab, or all of them in your system settings. Turning them off does not affect anything else.

Advertising

Inkbloom shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads never appear inside a puzzle. You will see them on menu screens, occasionally between practice puzzles, and as optional rewarded videos you choose to watch in exchange for hints or Ink Drops. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select ads. You can reset or limit that identifier in your device settings, and you can remove banner and interstitial ads permanently with a one-time purchase.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the App Store, with RevenueCat managing what you are entitled to. We never see or store your card details. We record only which entitlements your account holds and when a subscription expires.

How long we keep it

Your account and its progress are kept until you ask us to delete them. Leaderboard entries are kept as part of the historical record of each day. Crash reports and analytics are retained on Firebase's own schedule, which is capped at fourteen months for analytics data.

Your choices

  • Play anonymously. Do not link an account and do not set a display name.
  • Leave the leaderboards. Clear your display name and your times will rank without any name attached.
  • Turn off notifications, in the app or in system settings.
  • Limit ad tracking, in your device's privacy settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, by writing to sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com. We will confirm within thirty days. Deleting your account removes your profile, your progress and your streak.

If you are in the UK, the EU, or another region with similar law, you also have the right to correct your data, to object to how we process it, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Inkbloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to sriramgoparaju99@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and say so in the app before the change takes effect.
